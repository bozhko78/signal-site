The App

Built for the moments that feel too big.

Anchor is a free educational tool for people dealing with anxiety, panic attacks, OCD, social anxiety, and phobias. No subscriptions. No locked content. No data collection.

It doesn't diagnose or replace therapy — it explains what's happening in your body and mind, gives you grounded tools, and points you toward professional help when you're ready.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is simple.

Last updated: March 2026

We collect nothing.

Anchor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter in the app (thought records, exercise progress) stays entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

No accounts.

Anchor requires no account, no sign-up, and no login. We have no way to identify you.

No analytics.

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. There are no cookies, no device fingerprinting, and no behavioral tracking of any kind.

No ads.

Anchor is completely free with no advertisements. We are supported solely by optional donations from users who find the app helpful.

Donations.

Optional donations are processed entirely through Apple's App Store infrastructure (StoreKit). We do not handle or store any payment information.

Children.

Anchor does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, COPPA requirements are satisfied by design.

Medical disclaimer.

Anchor is an educational tool only. It does not provide medical advice, psychological diagnosis, or therapeutic treatment. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact emergency services or a crisis helpline immediately.
